BODYGUARD MOD - CONTROLS
========================
All keys can be changed in BodyguardMod.ini ([Keys] section).
Mod keys are ignored in the pause menu, on the phone, in cutscenes, while loading and while dead.


HOTKEYS
-------
F7    Open / close the menu
J     Attack: aim at a person or a vehicle, then press J. Everyone goes after it.
K     Regroup / cease fire. Everyone stops fighting and returns to you.
L     Driver:
        - no driver yet .............. calls your driver
        - sitting in the back ........ drives to your map waypoint
        - while he's driving you ..... toggles HURRY on/off (like a taxi)
U     Guard helicopter crews rappel down to you
F     Near your driver's car or the landed transport helicopter: get in the back


MENU NAVIGATION
---------------
Up / Down     or  Numpad 8 / 2    Move
Left / Right  or  Numpad 4 / 6    Change a value
Enter         or  Numpad 5        Select
Backspace     or  Numpad 0        Back / close
The box under the menu explains the selected option.


MENU PAGES
----------
Bodyguards      Model, count, Spawn, Dismiss All
Weapons         Choose a weapon, give it to all guards
Vehicle Follow  How guards who don't fit in your car follow you
                (Escort Car / Nearby Cars / Auto), escort model, catch-up teleport
Driver          Car, driver model, what he does when you get out
                (Stay in car / Guard car / Follow you), Hurry,
                Call driver, Go to waypoint, Stop / pull over, Dismiss driver
Motorcade       Lead cars, rear cars, guards per car, car model, crew model,
                When you drive (Lead to waypoint / Follow behind), Hurry (Off / On / Match driver),
                Get out when you stop, Form motorcade, Disband
Helicopters     Guard helis: model, count, Call, Rappel, Reinforce crew, Dismiss
                Transport: model, escort helis, after drop-off (Wait / Leave),
                Call pickup, Land now, Dismiss
Orders          Attack aimed target, Regroup / cease fire, Rappel heli guards, Dismiss All
Options         Invincible, Infinite ammo, Map blips, Auto-defend


QUICK HOW-TO
------------
Driver
  1. Press L (or Menu > Driver > Call driver). He drives up, honks and turns on his hazards.
  2. Walk up (the rear door opens) and press F to get in the back.
  3. Set a map waypoint. He drives you there. Press L during the trip to hurry.

Transport helicopter
  1. Stand in an open area. Menu > Helicopters > Call pickup.
  2. When it lands, press F to board.
  3. Set a map waypoint. It flies there and lands. Guard helis fly alongside.

Rappel
  1. Menu > Helicopters > Call guard helis. They circle over you.
  2. Press U. They hover above you and the crews rope down and join you.

Motorcade
  1. Get in a car (or have your driver pick you up).
  2. Menu > Motorcade > Form motorcade.
     - With your driver + a waypoint: the lead car drives the route, your car follows it, rears follow you.
     - Driving yourself: "Lead to waypoint" = a lead car drives to your waypoint and you follow it;
       "Follow behind" (or no waypoint) = every car chains behind you.
  3. Stop: everyone parks in formation at the curb. Guards stay in their cars unless there are hostiles
     (or "Get out when you stop" is on). Walk away from your parked car: 2 cars stay to guard it, the rest
     of the crews walk with you.

Attacking a vehicle
  Aim at a car and press J. Guards shoot the people inside; escort cars and
  rear motorcade cars chase it. Press K to call everyone off.
